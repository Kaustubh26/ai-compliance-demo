Sample Company Security Policy

1. Purpose
This security policy defines how the organization protects its information assets and complies with ISO 27001 and SOC 2.

2. Scope
The policy applies to all employees and contractors.

3. Statements
- Access to systems must be granted based on least privilege.
- All assets must have defined ownership.
- Information security roles should be clearly assigned.
